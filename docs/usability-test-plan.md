# Moderated MVP usability evidence

Status: **protocol ready; human sessions not yet run**.

## Scope and participants

Recruit five French-speaking participants who have compared or considered a rental property but have not used RentaLoc.
Use a fresh browser profile and fictional property figures. Do not record real addresses, income, tax identifiers, loan
files, exported JSON, or other personal financial information.

## Session tasks

1. From the landing page, explain in the participant's words what the product does and what it does not promise.
2. Enter a supplied furnished-property scenario, choose a plausible tax mode, and explain why that mode was selected.
3. Interpret the rating, after-tax cash flow, debt coverage, and break-even rent without moderator coaching.
4. Find one warning or methodological limitation that could change the decision.
5. Save the project, reload the page, restore it, export it, then delete all saved projects.
6. On a phone-sized viewport, find the advanced charge and fiscal inputs and explain what the collapsed summaries mean.

## Pass criteria

- At least 4/5 participants complete the core scenario without a blocking error.
- At least 4/5 correctly distinguish rating, yield, and after-tax cash flow.
- No participant mistakes the simplified tax estimate for filing or professional advice after reading the result.
- At least 4/5 select the supplied scenario's expected tax mode or recognize that professional confirmation is needed.
- All participants can find save/reload and at least 4/5 can find export and bulk deletion.
- No severe issue causes silent data loss, an irreversible unintended action, or a materially wrong investment conclusion.

Any severe issue reopens the corresponding P0/P1 item. Medium issues need an owner and disposition before MVP promotion.
